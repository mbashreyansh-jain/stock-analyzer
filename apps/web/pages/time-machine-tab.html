How the Time Machine works

Think of it as practising your trade 10,000 times before doing it once for real.

We look backWe take every trading day from ACME's last 3 years, about 780 days of real ups and downs, including big jumps and drops.
We shuffle themLike shuffling a deck of cards, we stitch those real days together in random order to make 10,000 different "next few weeks" that could happen. Some calm, some wild, each with a results day.
We play your trade in each oneIn every future we check: did the price reach your target first, hit your exit first, or neither? Then we count how it went across all 10,000.
