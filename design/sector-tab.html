How to read this chart
  • Left to right: how each industry is doing compared with NIFTY 50. Right of the middle line (RS-Ratio above 100) means beating the market.

  • The four boxes: Leading (beating the market and speeding up), Weakening (beating it but slowing), Lagging (behind and slowing), Improving (behind but catching up).
  • The tail is the last 5 weeks. Industries usually move clockwise: Improving, Leading, Weakening, Lagging, and round again.
